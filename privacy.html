01 / Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Sahil Yadav ("I", "me", "my", or "developer") handles information collected through this portfolio website (https://sahilyadav-portfolio.vercel.app/), associated source repositories, technical demonstrations, and direct professional communications.

As an independent full-stack developer and AI systems engineer, I respect personal privacy. This website is built without advertising trackers, behavioural profiling scripts, or marketing surveillance tools.

02 / Information Collected

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to communicate with you or ensure reliable site delivery:

  • Direct Inquiries & Communications: When you contact me via email (sahil.ydv8290@gmail.com), telephone, LinkedIn, or GitHub, I receive your contact details (e.g. name, email address, phone number) along with the content of your message and any accompanying files.
  • Hosting Infrastructure Telemetry: Like almost all web properties, standard server request logs (IP addresses, user agents, request timestamps, and referrer URLs) may be processed by hosting platforms such as Vercel solely for edge routing, DDoS prevention, rate-limiting, and server reliability diagnostics.
  • Local Theme Preferences: Your selected interface theme (dark or light mode) is saved in your browser’s local storage (under the key portfolio-theme) so your preference persists across sessions. This never leaves your device.

03 / Open-Source Projects & Desktop Software

This website features several open-source and desktop software architectures developed by Sahil Yadav. Each has distinct, privacy-preserving architectural guarantees:

F.R.I.D.A.Y. AI Assistant: F.R.I.D.A.Y. is built with a local-first philosophy. Speech-to-text (STT), text-to-speech (TTS), and Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool execution are executed directly on your workstation or routed through API keys you explicitly supply. The developer operates no central surveillance or voice telemetry servers.

SwiftDesk Remote Desktop Suite: SwiftDesk facilitates low-latency remote desktop streaming using peer-to-peer WebRTC connections with end-to-end encryption. Control input and file transfer payloads are transmitted directly between participating endpoints and are neither stored nor monitored by the developer.

For each individual open-source repository hosted on GitHub, consult the respective repository’s documentation and code for detailed technical implementation.

04 / How Information Is Used

Any information received is utilized solely for lawful, legitimate professional purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries, freelance opportunities, consulting projects, or job discussions.
  • To collaborate on open-source software, review bug reports, and merge pull requests.
  • To monitor site uptime, maintain edge caching, and protect against malicious cyber attacks.
  • To satisfy applicable legal, regulatory, or compliance obligations.

No Sale of Data: I do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties or data brokers under any circumstances.

05 / Cookies, Storage & Analytics

This website intentionally avoids intrusive third-party cookies:

  • Essential Local Storage: We store a single key portfolio-theme (set to dark or light) to remember your appearance preference.
  • Session Storage: A temporary session value is stored to cycle through greeting languages during your visit without repeating the same greeting twice consecutively.
  • No Tracking Cookies: No persistent third-party marketing or cross-site tracking cookies are injected into your browser.
